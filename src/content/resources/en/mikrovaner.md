**Atomic Habits** is James Clear's book about how habits actually form, and how you
can change them. It has sold many millions of copies worldwide, and appeared in
Norwegian as *Mikrovaner* (Pilar forlag, 2020).

## The main idea

We overestimate what we can manage in a day, and underestimate what small changes do
over years. Clear's point is that you should not bet on large, dramatic efforts – but
on habits so small they barely call on willpower. Over time those are the ones that
move you.

Another central point: **focus on the system, not just the goal.** It is not the goal
itself that produces results, but the routines that land you there almost
automatically.

## What you get out of it

The book is built around four simple principles for building good habits – make them
obvious, attractive, easy and satisfying – and their opposites for shedding bad ones.
All of it is concrete and easy to put into practice.

## Why it belongs here

Habits sit underneath almost everything we talk about on Spørretimen: how people last
in demanding jobs, how you get through a long degree, how you keep up the training or
the sleep routine. This is a practical way into exactly that.
