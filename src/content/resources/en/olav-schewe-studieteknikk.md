**Olav Schewe** is an author and researcher who works on how knowledge from cognitive
psychology and brain research can be used to learn better. He describes himself as a
thoroughly average pupil who became a top student at Oxford – with the help of good
study techniques.

## The books

- **Superstudent.** Norway's best-selling book on study technique, translated into a
  number of languages. A practical walk through note-taking, reading, memory, exam
  preparation and how to keep motivation up.
- **Superhjernen.** Learning strategies built on brain research and cognitive
  psychology. Nominated for the Brage Prize in 2020 and available in several
  languages. Published internationally as *Learn Like a Pro*, written with Barbara
  Oakley.
- **Jobbsøkerkoden.** A practical guide for job seekers.

## Why it is worth a look

Where memory technique is about remembering more, Schewe is about **the whole
learning process**: how you read, take notes, revise and prepare – and why some
methods work far better than others. A natural starting point for anyone facing a
demanding course of study.
