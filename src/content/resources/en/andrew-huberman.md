**Andrew Huberman** is a neuroscientist and professor at Stanford, and the person
behind **Huberman Lab** – one of the world's most popular knowledge podcasts about
health. He translates research on the brain and the body into concrete, practical
tools. The material is thorough, free, and an excellent place to start if you want
to dig deeper into the subjects we touch on in Spørretimen.

## What he covers

- Sleep and the circadian rhythm
- Focus, learning and productivity
- Dopamine, motivation and habits
- Training, strength and recovery
- Stress, breathing and the nervous system
- Light, diet, hormones and mental health

## Where to start

If you are new to it, the newsletter and the protocols are an easy way in – short,
practical summaries you can use straight away. If you want to go deeper, the podcast
has long, dedicated episodes on sleep among other things.

*The material is popular science and general knowledge – use it as inspiration and a
starting point, not as individual medical advice.*
