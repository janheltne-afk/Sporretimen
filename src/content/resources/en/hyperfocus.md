**Chris Bailey** is a Canadian author who writes about productivity and attention. In
*Hyperfocus: How to Be More Productive in a World of Distraction* (2018) he examines
how our attention actually works, and how it can be steered.

## What the book covers

- **Hyperfocus** – directing all your attention at one task at a time, and what it
  takes to get there and stay there.
- **Scatterfocus** – the opposite: deliberately letting the mind wander. Bailey
  argues this is not waste, but something the brain needs in order to connect ideas
  and rest.
- **Autopilot.** Much of the day passes without our choosing what we point our
  attention at. The first step is noticing where it actually goes.
- **How much we can hold at once**, and why tasks that are both boring and demanding
  are the hardest ones to stay with.
- **Practical moves** for removing distractions before they arise, rather than
  resisting them in the moment.

## Worth knowing

The book is practical and easy to read, with concrete exercises. Where *Stolen Focus*
looks at the surroundings and the systems around us, this one looks at the craft –
what you can do yourself. The two complement each other well, and read nicely
together.
