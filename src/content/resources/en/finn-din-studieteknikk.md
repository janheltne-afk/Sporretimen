One of John Erik's pieces of advice is that he wishes he had learned more about
study technique earlier. The main point: **there is no single right answer** –
different methods work for different people.

## Some things to try

- **Visual methods and memory palaces** – tie material to images and places (see the
  separate resource on memory palaces).
- **Active recall** – test yourself instead of simply reading it again.
- **Spaced repetition** – repeat at growing intervals over time.
- **Explain it to someone else** – saying a subject out loud reveals what you
  actually know.

## The point

Try different techniques on a specific subject, and notice what genuinely makes you
remember and understand better. That way you build a toolkit you can use for the rest
of your studies.
