Håvard Hasund is a paramedic who worked for several years in the ambulance service
in Oslo before moving home to Volda. That gives him an unusually good basis for
comparison: what is the real difference between working on an ambulance in a big
city and in a rural district?

Håvard has also taken part in the Canada expedition, and shares his experience of
safety, risk assessment and preparedness in the outdoors – seen through the eyes of
someone who works with emergency care every day.

Emergency medicine is discussed in general terms. No identifiable patient stories
are shared.
