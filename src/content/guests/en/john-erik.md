John Erik took his medical degree in Poland and has since worked with everything
from psychiatry to general practice, including in Northern Norway. On Spørretimen
he talks about the road into the profession, what an ordinary working day looks
like, and what the pressure and responsibility in the health service actually
involve.

Health information is handled responsibly, and no identifiable patient stories are
shared.
