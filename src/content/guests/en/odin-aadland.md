Odin Jean Aadland (born 1998) works in logistics and lives in Bergen. He studied
logistics and supply chain management at Molde University College, and works in
operational logistics.

On Spørretimen he talks about the hair transplant he had in Istanbul: how little
he looked into beforehand, what the day at the clinic actually involved, what the
anaesthetic did, what the trip cost, and how people at home reacted. It is an
unusually honest conversation, not least because he also talks about what he did
not do.

He is not a professional in this field. Everything he says is his own experience
of one procedure, at one clinic, at one point in time.
