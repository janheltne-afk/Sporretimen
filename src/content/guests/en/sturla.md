**Sturla Fagerli Larsen**, better known as the artist **Sturla**, is a songwriter
and performer from Orkdal in Trøndelag. Over a career spanning more than a decade
he has appeared on *The Voice*, fronted the band **22**, signed a record deal with
**Sony Music Norway** and taken part in *Melodi Grand Prix*, Norway's Eurovision
selection.

Sturla is known for Norwegian-language pop sung in the Trøndelag dialect, and is
behind songs such as “Trondheim”, “Skår i hjertet”, “67” and “50”. He has also
collaborated with **Hanne Mjøen**, and his music has been playlisted on both NRK P1
and NRK P3.

But the career has not followed a straight line. After several years in the music
industry Sturla moved back to his home village and started working shifts at a
smelting plant, while continuing to write songs and play concerts. What he learned
from the record business, from television, from ordinary working life and from
going it alone as an independent artist has since become an important part of his
music.
