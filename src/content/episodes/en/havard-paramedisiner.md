What does working as a paramedic actually involve? Håvard Hasund has several years
behind him in the Oslo ambulance service, and now works in Vanylven and at the
out-of-hours clinic in southern Sunnmøre. That makes him one of the few people who
can genuinely compare the two working lives.

We talk about the road into the profession – from the Red Cross and smoke diving in
the Coast Guard to the paramedic degree at OsloMet – what the training and the
placements are really like, and why the first placement almost made him quit. We also
get into how the ambulance service looks after its people after difficult callouts,
the year he spent as a medical dispatcher, and what separates the pace of the city
from the long distances and quiet shifts of the districts.

*Emergency medicine is discussed in general terms. No identifiable patient stories
are shared.*
