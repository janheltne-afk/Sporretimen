Sturla has covered a lot of ground in the Norwegian music industry: X Factor, Idol,
The Voice, Melodi Grand Prix and several years under a record deal with Sony Music.
Today he runs everything himself, with a goal of playing 100 concerts during 2026.

We talk about the road through the television competitions, what a record deal
actually involves, and why he eventually chose to leave it. And we go properly into
the business most listeners never hear about: how the Norwegian royalty societies
Tono and Gramo work, how little streaming actually pays, and how an artist books,
prices and delivers their own concerts.

*The content is Sturla's own experience and his own assessment of the industry he
works in, not general advice about money or copyright.*
