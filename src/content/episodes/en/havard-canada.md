In the summer of 2025 Håvard Hasund and four others paddled **1,500 kilometres**
through the wilderness of northern Canada – from Yellowknife to Baker Lake, in 47
days, without seeing another person on the way.

We go through the whole trip: where the idea came from, how it was planned over a
year and a half, and what they brought in the way of safety equipment, firearms and
medicine. And then what actually happened – the fishing, the musk ox, the
five-kilometre portage, the shotgun that disappeared into a tarn, the rapids, and the
grizzly sow that rose onto her hind legs thirty metres away.

Håvard is a paramedic, so we also talk about how you assess risk when help is hours
away – and why capsizing is far more dangerous than a bear.

*The content is experience from a single trip, not a substitute for qualified
training in outdoor skills, first aid or whitewater paddling.*
